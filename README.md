# Phishing Simulation & Security Awareness Campaign

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the design and execution of a controlled **Phishing Simulation and Security Awareness Campaign** across five organisational departments: IT, Sales, Procurement, Audit, and Marketing.

The campaign was conducted in alignment with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A Control 6.3 — Information Security Awareness, Education and Training**, with the objective of evaluating employee susceptibility to phishing attacks and measuring the effectiveness of security awareness training.

## 🎯 Objective

The project followed a three-stage methodology:

- **Baseline Simulation:** Establish initial phishing susceptibility and reporting metrics.
- **Security Awareness Training:** Deliver targeted phishing awareness training to employees.
- **Post-Training Simulation:** Measure improvements in employee behaviour and security awareness.

## 🧪 Simulation & Assessment

The phishing simulation infrastructure was established using **Zphisher** on Kali Linux.

A simulated LinkedIn login page was used as part of a controlled social engineering exercise to evaluate employee responses to phishing emails.

The assessment measured:

- Credential submission rates
- Phishing email reporting rates
- Employee susceptibility
- Pre-training and post-training performance
- Security awareness improvement

## 📊 Key Results

Across the three departments with complete before-and-after data:

| Department | Key Outcome |
|---|---|
| IT | 100% reduction in credential submissions; reporting increased by 650% |
| Sales | 100% reduction in credential submissions; reporting increased by 3,000% |
| Marketing | 100% reduction in credential submissions; 35 phishing reports recorded |

These results demonstrate measurable improvements in phishing awareness and employee reporting behaviour following the training programme.
